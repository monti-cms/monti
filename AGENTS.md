Monti's core (`packages/core`) is being rewritten from scratch, and its design is `docs/refactor/core.md`. That document and what the owner says are the only authority. The old code, `docs/design/features` and the old tracker are gone and were wrong where they differ: do not rebuild anything from memory of them. The other documents in `docs/refactor` say what goes to the other packages and are worked on only after the core. Before any work, read the stage you are in (core.md, "작업 방식"). The acceptance conditions are in `docs/acceptance/core/` and are read-only for you; how they are written and how tests link to them is in `docs/acceptance/README.md`. Keep acceptance conditions concise and focused on required domain rules (do not pre-emptively inflate them with speculative edge cases; split large resources into cohesive slices). Each condition has one test whose title starts with its number, and you add nothing the conditions do not state; Stryker survivors are classified into 4 buckets (ignore meaningless/equivalent, delete unrequested branch, strengthen existing AC, or escalate new rule to the owner). Do not defer or shrink work: if something is blocked, too big, or a condition looks wrong, report it to the PM.

Docs under `docs/` are written in Korean: the owner reads and approves them. Code names, file paths, tokens and commands in them stay as they are in the code. Everything else is in English (code comments, test titles, READMEs, error messages, commits, PRs); Korean appears elsewhere only in the `ko` message dictionaries, test data, Storybook stories and `README.ko.md`. When a `README.md` changes, update its `README.ko.md` in the same change.

Commit messages and PR titles follow Conventional Commits (`type(scope): subject`). The scopes are in `commitlint.config.mjs`; a new package adds its own.

Prefer LSP for symbol navigation, references, types, rename and diagnostics.
